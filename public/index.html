Card2Leads

Business Card Scanning & Contact Management

Card2Leads helps businesses and professionals turn business cards into organised digital contacts. Scan or upload business cards, extract names, phone numbers, email addresses, companies and designations, review and correct the details, add labels and voice notes, and export your records as Excel, CSV or VCF files.

You can also optionally connect your Google account to save selected contacts to Google Contacts or create and update contact records in Google Sheets. Connecting Google is optional and is only requested when you choose to use a feature that needs it.

Card2Leads is operated by BrillBrains Consultants Pvt. Ltd.

What you can do with Card2Leads

  • Scan a business card with your camera or upload single and bulk card images.
  • Automatically extract contact details from each card.
  • Review and correct the extracted information before saving.
  • Add labels, notes and voice notes to organise your leads.
  • Export contacts as Excel, CSV or VCF files.
  • Optionally sync selected contacts to Google Contacts and Google Sheets.
