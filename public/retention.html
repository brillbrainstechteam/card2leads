Data Retention Policy

The default workspace policy is to retain card images for 90 days so users can verify extraction quality and resolve mistakes.

Voice-note audio is retained while the related card or contact remains active so users can replay the original note. The raw transcript and structured fields are stored with the contact for exports and follow-up.

Saved contacts and audit records are retained until the user deletes the account or until a formal retention period is configured for the production business.

Before selling, define the exact retention period, backup deletion window, and support process for permanent deletion requests.
