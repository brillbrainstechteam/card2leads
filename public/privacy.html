Privacy Policy

Card2Leads by BrillBrains Consultants Pvt. Ltd.

Last updated: 04 August 2026

BrillBrains Consultants Pvt. Ltd. (“BrillBrains”, “Card2Leads”, “we”, “us” or “our”) respects your privacy.

Card2Leads is a business-card digitisation and lead-management tool available through the Card2Leads website. It helps users extract information from business cards, convert that information into structured tables, add information through voice input, organise contacts using labels, export records, and synchronise selected information with Google Contacts and Google Sheets.

This Privacy Policy explains:

  • The information we collect
  • Information relating to people whose business cards are uploaded
  • How we use information
  • Google account and Google API data
  • Voice, OCR and AI processing
  • How we share information
  • Data retention and deletion
  • Data security
  • Your rights and choices
  • Cookies and technical information
  • International data processing
  • Children’s privacy
  • Changes to this policy
  • Contact and grievance details

This Privacy Policy applies specifically to Card2Leads. Other products and services operated by BrillBrains Consultants Pvt. Ltd. may be governed by separate privacy policies.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide, information you upload for processing, information received through integrations that you authorise, and limited technical information generated when you use Card2Leads.

1.1 Account and profile information

When you create or use a Card2Leads account, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Mobile number
  • Company or organisation name
  • Account login and authentication information
  • Subscription, plan or usage information, where applicable
  • Preferences and account settings
  • Communications sent to our support team

Where Google Sign-In is used, we may receive basic profile information such as your name, email address, profile image and Google account identifier.

We do not receive or store your Google account password.

1.2 Business-card images and uploaded files

When you scan or upload a business card, we may process:

  • Images of the front and back of the business card
  • Photographs captured using your device camera
  • Images uploaded from your device
  • Bulk-uploaded business-card images
  • Files containing business-card or contact information

Business cards may contain information such as:

  • Name
  • Company or organisation
  • Job title or designation
  • Mobile and telephone numbers
  • Email addresses
  • Business and postal addresses
  • Website addresses
  • Social-media or professional-profile links
  • QR-code information
  • Product, service or business category
  • Other information printed on the card

1.3 Extracted and structured contact information

Card2Leads converts uploaded content into structured records. These records may include:

  • Name
  • Mobile number
  • Telephone number
  • Email address
  • Company name
  • Designation
  • Address
  • Website
  • Notes
  • Labels and tags
  • Contact category or importance
  • Event or exhibition name
  • Event or exhibition date
  • Lead source
  • User-added comments
  • Other custom fields selected or entered by the user

Users can review, correct, add to or remove extracted information before exporting or synchronising it.

1.4 Voice inputs and recordings

When you use voice-input features, we may process:

  • Audio recordings captured through your microphone
  • Voice notes uploaded by you
  • Transcriptions generated from the audio
  • Labels, notes, names, categories or contact details spoken by you
  • Bulk voice instructions associated with multiple contacts

Microphone access is requested only when you choose to use a voice-enabled feature.

You should avoid including confidential, sensitive or unrelated personal information in voice inputs.

1.5 Google Contacts information

When you choose to connect Google Contacts, Card2Leads may access the Google Contacts data necessary to:

  • Create new contacts
  • Add extracted business-card information to contacts
  • Update a contact selected by you
  • Apply labels or identifying information
  • Check for matching or duplicate contacts, where this feature is enabled
  • Display the result of a contact-sync request

We access Google Contacts only after you provide permission through Google’s authorisation process.

1.6 Google Sheets and Google Drive information

When you choose to synchronise data with Google Sheets, Card2Leads may access the information necessary to:

  • Create a spreadsheet selected or requested by you
  • Create or select a worksheet
  • Add rows containing extracted contact data
  • Update data previously added through Card2Leads
  • Read spreadsheet names, worksheet names, identifiers or relevant rows when required to complete a user-requested operation
  • Prevent duplicate entries, where this feature is enabled

Card2Leads does not intentionally access unrelated Google Drive files or spreadsheets that are not required for a feature requested by you.

We do not access Gmail, Google Calendar or other unrelated Google services through the Card2Leads integration.

1.7 Export and download information

Card2Leads allows users to generate and download:

  • Microsoft Excel files
  • CSV files
  • VCF or vCard files
  • Structured contact tables
  • Other supported contact-data formats

After a file has been downloaded to your device or transferred to another system, you are responsible for protecting and lawfully using that copy.

1.8 Technical and usage information

When you use Card2Leads, we may automatically receive limited technical information, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Date and time of access
  • Session information
  • Feature usage
  • Error and diagnostic logs
  • Upload, extraction, synchronisation and export status
  • Security and fraud-prevention information

This information is used to operate, secure, troubleshoot and improve the service.

Information separately retained in your Card2Leads account will remain until you delete it or request account deletion.

5. Voice, OCR and AI Processing

Card2Leads may use optical character recognition, transcription and artificial-intelligence technologies to extract and organise information.

Business-card images, voice recordings and relevant instructions may be processed by carefully selected infrastructure, OCR, transcription or AI service providers solely to provide the requested extraction or transcription result.

Such processing may involve:

  • Reading printed text from an image
  • Identifying likely names, phone numbers, companies and email addresses
  • Converting spoken information into text
  • Standardising different contact formats
  • Mapping extracted information into table columns
  • Identifying possible duplicate or incomplete records
  • Suggesting corrections for user review

Automated extraction may occasionally be inaccurate. Users should review information before exporting it, synchronising it or using it to contact another individual.

We do not guarantee that extracted information will be complete or error-free.

We do not use Google Workspace API data or identifiable Card2Leads user content to train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Card2Leads, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and protect the service.

7.1 Recommended retention periods

Unless a different period is required by law or communicated within the product:

  • Temporary business-card images and voice recordings

Temporary processing copies are generally deleted after extraction and verification or within 30 days of processing.

Where Card2Leads allows users to save original images or audio in their workspace, those files may remain until the user deletes them or closes the account.

  • Extracted contact records, tables, notes and labels

These records are retained while the user’s account remains active or until the user deletes them.

Following account deletion, active copies are generally deleted within 30 days, subject to legal, security and backup requirements.

  • Google OAuth tokens

OAuth tokens are retained while the Google integration remains connected. They are deleted, revoked or rendered unusable after the user disconnects Google access, revokes permission or deletes the Card2Leads account.

  • Generated export files

Export files may be generated temporarily and deleted from our systems after delivery or within 30 days. Copies downloaded to the user’s device are controlled by the user.

  • Support and account communications

Support records may be retained for up to three years or for another period required to resolve an issue, comply with law or establish legal claims.

  • Security, access and diagnostic logs

Security and operational logs may be retained for up to 12 months or for a longer period where required by applicable law or necessary to investigate an incident.

  • Backups

Deleted information may remain in encrypted or access-restricted backups for up to 90 days before being overwritten or permanently removed through the normal backup cycle.

7.2 Account deletion

You may request deletion of your Card2Leads account and associated personal information by contacting us using the details below or through an account-deletion option, where available.

Before completing a deletion request, we may verify your identity and authority over the relevant account.

Deletion does not automatically remove:

  • Contacts already created in your Google Contacts account;
  • Information already written to Google Sheets;
  • Files downloaded to your device;
  • Files shared with another person or system; or

Information that another Card2Leads user independently obtained and uploaded.

You must delete those copies from the relevant external service or device.

We may retain limited information where required for legal compliance, fraud prevention, security investigations, dispute resolution or enforcement of agreements.
