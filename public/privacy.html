Privacy Policy

Card2Leads

Operated by BrillBrains Consultants Pvt. Ltd.

Last updated: 12 August 2026

BrillBrains Consultants Pvt. Ltd. (“BrillBrains”, “we”, “us” or “our”) operates Card2Leads, a business-card digitisation, contact-management and lead-organisation application.

This Privacy Policy applies specifically to Card2Leads and explains how information is collected, accessed, used, processed, stored, shared and deleted when you use the Card2Leads website and related features.

About Card2Leads

Card2Leads helps businesses and professionals convert physical or digital business cards into organised digital contact records.

Depending on the features you choose to use, Card2Leads may allow you to:

  • Scan or upload business cards;
  • Extract information such as names, mobile numbers, email addresses, companies and designations;
  • Review and correct extracted information;
  • Organise contacts using labels, categories, events and notes;
  • Add information through voice input;
  • Export contact records to supported file formats;
  • Save or synchronise selected contact information with Google Contacts; and
  • Create or update Google Sheets containing contact information when requested by you.

Connecting a Google account is optional. Card2Leads requests access to Google services only when you choose to use a feature that requires such access and after you provide permission through Google's authorisation process.

Other products or services operated by BrillBrains Consultants Pvt. Ltd. may be governed by separate privacy policies.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly, information you upload for processing, information received through integrations you authorise, and limited technical information generated when you use Card2Leads.

1.1 Account and Profile Information

When you create or use a Card2Leads account, we may collect:

  • Email address;

  • Account login and authentication information;

Where Google Sign-In is used, we may receive basic profile information authorised by you, such as:

We do not receive or store your Google account password.

1.2 Business-Card Images and Uploaded Files

When you scan or upload a business card, Card2Leads may process:

  • Images of the front or back of a business card;
  • Photographs captured using your device camera;
  • Images uploaded from your device;
  • Bulk-uploaded business-card images; and
  • Files containing business-card or contact information.

Business cards may contain information such as:

  • Name;
  • Company or organisation;
  • Job title or designation;
  • Mobile and telephone numbers;
  • Email addresses;
  • Business or postal addresses;
  • Website addresses;
  • Social-media or professional-profile information;
  • QR-code information;
  • Product, service or business category; and
  • Other information appearing on the card.

1.3 Extracted and Structured Contact Information

Card2Leads may convert uploaded content into structured contact records containing information such as:

  • Name;
  • Mobile number;
  • Telephone number;
  • Email address;
  • Company name;
  • Designation;
  • Address;
  • Website;
  • Notes;
  • Labels and tags;
  • Contact category or importance;
  • Event or exhibition name;
  • Event or exhibition date;
  • Lead source;
  • User-added comments; and
  • Other custom fields entered or selected by the user.

Users can review, correct, add to or remove extracted information before exporting or synchronising it.

1.4 Voice Inputs and Recordings

When you voluntarily use a voice-enabled feature, Card2Leads may process:

  • Audio captured through your microphone;
  • Voice notes uploaded by you;
  • Transcriptions generated from audio;
  • Labels, notes, names, categories or contact details spoken by you; and
  • Voice instructions associated with one or more contact records.

Microphone access is requested only when you choose to use a voice-enabled feature.

You should avoid including confidential, sensitive or unrelated personal information in voice inputs.

When you explicitly connect Google Contacts or request a Google Contacts synchronisation feature, Card2Leads may access Google Contacts information necessary to perform the operation requested by you.

  • Identify possible matching or duplicate contacts where this functionality is enabled; and

Google Contacts data is not accessed for advertising, profiling or unrelated purposes.

2.3 Google Sheets Information

When you choose to export, create or synchronise information using Google Sheets, Card2Leads may access information necessary to complete the operation requested by you.

Depending on the permissions granted, this may include the ability to:

  • Read relevant rows when necessary to perform a user-requested synchronisation; and
  • Detect possible duplicate records where this functionality is enabled.

Card2Leads uses such access only to perform functionality initiated, selected or authorised by the user.

2.4 Google Drive Access

Where a Google Sheets feature requires Google Drive-related permissions, Card2Leads uses such permissions only as necessary to create, locate, select or operate on files relevant to the feature requested by you and within the permissions granted through Google.

Card2Leads does not intentionally browse, collect or use unrelated files from your Google Drive for unrelated purposes.

Card2Leads does not use Google Drive information for advertising, behavioural profiling, data brokerage or unrelated analytics.

12. Disconnecting or Revoking Google Access

You control whether Card2Leads remains connected to your Google account.

You may also revoke Card2Leads' access through the third-party application or connection controls available within your Google Account.

After access is revoked:

  • Card2Leads will no longer be able to perform new Google operations requiring the revoked permission;
  • OAuth credentials associated with the disconnected integration will be deleted, revoked or rendered unusable within a reasonable period;
  • Contacts already created within your Google Contacts account will remain there unless you delete them;
  • Information already written to Google Sheets will remain within those spreadsheets unless you delete it; and
  • Information separately stored within your Card2Leads account may remain until you delete the relevant records or request account deletion.

Revoking Google access does not automatically delete information already created in your Google account because that information is controlled through the relevant Google service.
