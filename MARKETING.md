# Card2Leads — Product & Marketing Reference

> Working title: **Card2Leads by BrillBrains**. A candidate rename under consideration is **LeadSnap**.
> This document describes what the product actually does today, plus positioning and USPs, so marketing content (website, ads, decks, WhatsApp/Insta creatives) can be built from a single source of truth.

---

## 1. One-line pitch

**Turn a stack of exhibition business cards into clean, ready-to-use contacts — in Excel, Google Sheets, or straight on your phone — with follow-up notes and team assignment built in.**

## 2. Who it's for

- **Exhibitors and sales teams** at trade shows and expos (jewellery, textiles, food, manufacturing, B2B) — built with Indian exhibitions like **IIJS** and **GJEPC** in mind.
- Anyone who collects **many business cards fast** and needs them as an organised, shareable follow-up list — not a shoebox of cards.
- Teams where **multiple people** collect cards and someone needs to **route leads for follow-up**.

## 3. The core problem it solves

At an exhibition you collect dozens or hundreds of cards. Afterwards they sit unused because manually typing them into a sheet is slow and error-prone, context ("what did they want?") is forgotten, and there's no clean way to split follow-ups across the team. Card2Leads removes that entire gap between **collecting** cards and **acting** on them.

## 4. What the product does (features)

### 4.1 Capture
- **Batch upload** — add up to 20 card photos at once from any device.
- **Mobile camera scan** — snap cards one at a time on a phone.
- **Front & back** — optionally add the back of a card when it has extra details (second number, address, QR); both sides are saved as one contact.

### 4.2 AI extraction
- Reads each card and pulls out **name, phone number(s), company, designation, email, website, address, and more** into clean fields — automatically.
- Powered by leading AI models with an **optional second verification pass** for higher accuracy on important batches.
- Handles messy, real-world cards; flags anything low-confidence for a human to check.

### 4.3 Review before saving
- **Confidence flags** highlight uncertain fields so you fix only what needs fixing.
- **Duplicate detection** stops the same contact being saved twice.
- Fast inline edits — approve a contact in seconds.

### 4.4 Voice notes (context that closes deals)
- Record a quick **voice note per contact** — "wanted bridal sets, follow up after Diwali."
- Works in **Hindi, English, or a mix (Hinglish)** and saves the **transcript** with the contact.

### 4.5 Organise by exhibition
- Every contact is tagged with its **exhibition name and year** (e.g. "GJEPC 2026").
- Keep **separate lists per exhibition/event**; switch between them anytime.
- **Search** by name, number, or company, and **filter** by exhibition or by the team member a contact is assigned to.

### 4.6 Team follow-up assignment
- Add your **team members by name** (no extra logins needed).
- **Assign any contact** to a team member for follow-up, right from the contact list.
- Filter to "show me everyone assigned to Priya" or "show unassigned" so nothing slips.

### 4.7 Save & export anywhere (not locked in)
- **Excel (.xlsx)** and **CSV** downloads — sales-ready columns.
- **Google Sheets** — create one live sheet per exhibition that stays updated as you add/edit contacts.
- **Google Contacts** — save contacts into an **exhibition-named group** on your phone (create/update only; the app never deletes your contacts).
- **VCF file** — one file the whole team can import into their phones; contacts stay grouped by exhibition.

### 4.8 Accounts, security & billing
- **Email or Google sign-in**; email verification and password reset built in.
- **Shared workspace model** — anyone with the workspace's credentials works in the same organisation with equal access.
- **Security**: hashed passwords, CSRF protection, rate limiting, encrypted Google tokens, strict per-workspace data isolation.
- **Plans by scan volume** (see below), with a free trial and top-up credits.

## 5. Unique selling points (USPs)

1. **Purpose-built for exhibitions, not generic OCR.** Exhibition/year labeling, per-event lists, and team follow-up are first-class — not bolted on.
2. **India-first.** Hindi/Hinglish voice notes, INR pricing, and designed around shows like IIJS and GJEPC.
3. **Context, not just contacts.** Voice notes capture *why* a lead matters — the difference between a name and a deal.
4. **Save it your way.** Excel, CSV, Google Sheets (live), Google Contacts, or a shareable VCF — you're never locked into one format.
5. **Built for teams.** Assign leads to the right person and track who owns what — without buying a seat per person.
6. **Fast and forgiving.** Batch upload, AI extraction, confidence flags, and duplicate detection turn hundreds of cards into a clean list quickly.
7. **No installs required.** Works in the browser on desktop and mobile; contacts sync straight to phones via Google or VCF.
8. **Affordable, pay-for-what-you-scan.** Simple scan-credit plans with a free trial and top-ups — no per-seat pricing.

## 7. How it works (3 steps — use verbatim in creatives)

1. **Add card photos** — pick from your device or take a photo.
2. **We read the details** — name, number, and company, automatically.
3. **Check and save** — fix anything, then save to your list, sheet, or phone.
